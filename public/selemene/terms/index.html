Purpose & scope

Selemene Engine is a symbolic‑reflection companion within the Tryambakam Noesis umbrella. This draft covers its planned ChatGPT integration for engine discovery, input preparation and explicitly requested readings. It does not establish that a customer web account interface is available.

Nature of readings

Readings offered through Selemene are symbolic reflection. They are not medical, psychological, clinical, financial, or legal advice, and they do not guarantee outcomes. Interpretation is written on top of calculated values and should be read as an occasion for reflection, not instruction.

If you are looking for clinical assessment or professional advice, please seek a qualified practitioner in the relevant field.

Your inputs & accuracy

Calculations are only as accurate as the inputs you provide. When information is missing — for example, an unknown birth time — the preparation step should identify required missing fields before any calculation. It will not invent missing details, and neither should you: prefer unknown over a guess entered as fact.

Account & permitted access

You are responsible for the account you use to access Selemene, including keeping any credentials confidential. Access is granted for the purposes described in these terms and in the accompanying privacy draft.

Use only the account permissions granted to you. Do not attempt to bypass authentication, access restrictions or rate limits. Final permitted-use terms require publisher review.

Calculations & timeouts

A calculation or combined reading may save supplied inputs and returned results and update account state. Saving is asynchronous and a successful response does not guarantee that a history record was saved (see the privacy draft).

If a calculation appears to time out or the result is ambiguous, do not click retry repeatedly. Selemene does not automatically re‑execute an ambiguous run, and repeated retries may create duplicate history entries or count against usage. Treat the outcome as uncertain and check available account history before deciding whether to request another reading.

Service availability

Selemene is under active development. Availability can vary between engines, workflows, and surfaces. When a workflow completes only partially — for example, some steps succeed while others fail or are skipped — the result will be flagged as partial rather than presented as complete.

No guarantee of correctness

A calculation, interpretation or workflow output can be incomplete or incorrect. Final warranty and liability terms require publisher review. Please treat outputs as material for reflection and cross‑check anything you intend to rely upon.
