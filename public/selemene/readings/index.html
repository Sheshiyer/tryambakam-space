How a reading works

Prepare, request, reflect

  1. Discover. In a host with the Selemene plugin available, ask which engines or workflows are currently supported. No personal Selemene account, API key, or OAuth approval is needed for the free connector.
  2. Prepare. State your question and supply only the fields the chosen engine needs. Missing required date, time, coordinates, or timezone is requested rather than invented. Preparation alone does not run a calculation.
  3. Review storage. Read the shared-account disclosure below before asking for an ordinary calculation or workflow.
  4. Request. Explicitly ask to run the prepared reading. Errors, partial outputs, unavailable engines, and missing source material stay visible.
  5. Reflect. ChatGPT's current model writes interpretation and final prose under your applicable host limits. Aletheios and Pichet offer complementary Witness writing perspectives inside that conversation.

Selemene supplies engine calculations, local Tarot and I Ching draws, static framework text, and reviewed rule-based synthesis. This free connector invokes no additional operator-funded narration model. Document exports depend on host-native tools actually available.

Before you request a calculation

Shared service storage

Free readings use a shared Selemene administrator account. Birth inputs and calculated results may be stored there; usage, profile, XP and phase may change. There is no separate private user history.

Ordinary readings attempt asynchronous persistence. A successful calculation is not proof of storage, and the connector provides no private personal history or deletion tool. The separate chart handler does not itself save reading history; this does not establish end-to-end no-storage processing. Read the privacy notice for data requests and retention limits.

Evidence and interpretation

Keep source limits visible

  • I Ching. Six bottom-to-top cast lines determine the King Wen primary and relating hexagrams consistently. All 64 primary judgments and Great Symbolism images have provenance to an original James Legge 1882 scan. Moving-line passages are not supplied; legacy names, Chinese text, and general meanings can be incomplete or unverified.
  • Gene Keys. Activation values preserve the actual Human Design gate, line, and source, including Sun/Earth activation spheres. Complete verified meanings remain incomplete or unverified; physiological effects are not measured.
  • Vedic chart. Returned D1/D9 planetary and house fields do not supply advanced yogas, dignities, combustion findings, Atmakaraka, or Darakaraka. An absent field is not a verified negative finding.

Calculated values, source text, and host-written interpretation have different authority. Symbolic reflection does not provide medical, psychological, financial, or legal advice, and does not guarantee outcomes.

Free access has bounds

Quotas, timeouts, and support

Defaults allow 120 requests and 30 calculation attempts per connecting IP per minute; 600 requests and 60 calculation attempts for the service per minute; and 1,000 calculation attempts for the service per UTC day. These configurable limits are shared abuse controls, not personal allowances. ChatGPT gateways or shared networks can share a quota.

Later validation or backend failure can still count as a calculation attempt. Do not repeatedly retry an ambiguous timeout: its persistence outcome may be uncertain. Use returned retry guidance and the support page for help.
