What Selemene is

Purpose & posture

Selemene Engine is a symbolic-reflection companion. Its planned ChatGPT integration helps you discover supported engines, understand their inputs, and request a reading using information you choose to provide.

Two things happen inside a reading, and they are kept distinct:

  • Calculation — the values or symbolic selections actually returned by the selected engine.
  • Interpretation — symbolic language written on top of those values, offered as reflection rather than instruction.

Selemene will not fabricate missing details (for example, an unknown birth time). Preparation identifies missing required fields before any calculation. You can supply them or choose a supported reading that does not need them.

ChatGPT integration

In preparation

The ChatGPT integration is in preparation. It is not currently available or live, and there is no install action on this page. This draft describes the intended shape of the initial release so that policy, terms, and support text can be reviewed against a concrete surface.

The initial release is being prepared around seven capabilities. Account connection and the available engines still need verification in ChatGPT.

  1. 01Discover engines
  2. 02Understand an engine
  3. 03Prepare your inputs
  4. 04Request one reading
  5. 05Discover combined readings
  6. 06Understand a combined reading
  7. 07Request a combined reading

No admin, billing, media, Suno, or full witness‑report tools are part of the initial release. A live count of engines exposed through the plugin is not fixed at this stage.

How it behaves

Preparation vs. calculation

When you talk to Selemene about a reading, it will first prepare — restating what you asked for and what inputs it needs. A reading runs only when you explicitly request it. Calculations can save supplied inputs and results and may update your account profile, history, usage, XP or phase.

Calculated values are shown as values. Interpretation, when offered, is framed as symbolic reflection. Selemene does not provide medical, psychological, financial, or legal advice, and does not guarantee outcomes.

Where things live

Boundary at a glance

  • A Cloudflare Worker is the intended MCP / OAuth boundary between ChatGPT and Selemene.
  • Selemene itself is hosted on Railway and handles calculation and account state.
  • ChatGPT applies its own provider policies to anything you type there; we do not restate or replace them here.
