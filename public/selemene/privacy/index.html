Scope of this draft

Selemene Engine is a symbolic‑reflection companion within the Tryambakam Noesis umbrella. This draft describes the privacy behaviour intended for its initial ChatGPT integration. The ChatGPT integration is in preparation and is not currently available or live.

This draft is not a legal notice. It is not effective. Nothing here overrides ChatGPT's own provider policies or any future signed agreement between Selemene and its users.

Information you may provide

Depending on which tool you use, Selemene may receive information that you type or paste yourself. Not every tool needs every field; several tools work with far less.

  • Name, when required by the selected engine.
  • Birth date, birth time, birth place, coordinates, and timezone — when relevant to a specific engine.
  • The question or intent you bring to a reading.
  • Calculation inputs (parameters requested by a specific engine) and the calculated results those inputs produce.

Selemene does not attempt to fill in missing personal details on your behalf. If a required birth time is unknown, preparation should identify it as missing before calculation. A reading that does not require it can be considered instead.

How that information is used

When you explicitly ask for a calculation or a workflow, Selemene will use the values you provided to compute results. Those inputs and results may be saved to your Selemene account so that:

  • You can revisit prior readings and their history.
  • Your profile, usage counters, XP, or current phase can be updated as designed by the engine you invoked.

The current reading store can hold the supplied inputs, returned results, reflection prompt, account association and timestamps. Saving is asynchronous and can fail, so a successful calculation does not guarantee that its history record was saved. There is no verified ephemeral or "no‑storage" mode.

Where processing happens

Selemene processing uses the following intended infrastructure; this is not a complete processor inventory:

  • A Cloudflare Worker is the intended MCP / OAuth boundary — the surface through which ChatGPT reaches Selemene tools.
  • Selemene itself is hosted on Railway and is where calculation and account state are processed and stored.

The complete processor inventory, hosting regions and cross‑border transfer arrangements need verification and disclosure before publication.

ChatGPT as the connecting surface

When you connect Selemene from within ChatGPT, your ChatGPT conversation is governed by ChatGPT's own privacy and provider policies. Anything you type into ChatGPT is subject to those policies before it reaches Selemene. The final policy must identify how the connecting service and Selemene each handle the information shared through the integration.

Authorization & credentials

Any authorization credential used to link Selemene to ChatGPT is entered only through the secure browser linking form provided by Selemene. Do not paste tokens, API keys, or passwords into a chat window or into any public support channel.

The demo key of the form tn_free_… generated by the website terminal is a demo string, not a valid credential. Treat it accordingly.

Retention & deletion (pending)

The current API includes account-profile access and reading-history retrieval. A general export function and a customer account or reading deletion endpoint were not found in the source review. No timed purge of reading, profile or usage records was found. Retention periods, provider log and backup lifetimes, and an operational request-and-deletion process must be established before publication.

Security posture

Security disclosures must be checked against the deployed integration before this policy takes effect. The current API request middleware records request metadata including method, path, trace identifier, account identifier, response status and duration; this does not establish every provider logging practice. Provider retention and backup arrangements remain under review.

Before publication

Items pending publisher decisions

  • Legal identity of the data controller (entity name, address, jurisdiction).
  • Contact channel for privacy questions, access, and deletion requests.
  • Retention periods for account data, calculation history, and backups.
  • Complete list of subprocessors and their locations.
  • Cross‑border transfer mechanisms and applicable safeguards.
  • Concrete deletion workflow and verification of completeness.
  • Minimum age of use, country availability, and any commerce terms.
  • Effective date and versioning policy for this notice.
