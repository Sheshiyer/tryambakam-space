Information used for a reading

You choose the reading and the information you provide. Depending on the engine, tool inputs can include a name, birth date and time, coordinates, timezone, calculation date, self-reported selections, or a question for a symbolic draw. Required missing inputs are identified during preparation; an unknown birth time should not be invented.

The information sent in a tool request is used to prepare or perform the requested calculation and return its result. The free connector creates no personal Selemene login and does not require your API key or OAuth approval. Its sponsor credential stays in a server secret.

ChatGPT writes explanations and interpretation using your current host model and applicable ChatGPT limits. Optional writing context can remain in your conversation unless a tool needs it. This connector does not send a narration request to an additional operator-funded model service.

Shared account and storage

Free readings use a shared Selemene administrator account. Birth inputs and calculated results may be stored there; usage, profile, XP and phase may change. There is no separate private user history.

Ordinary engine and workflow handlers attempt to save supplied inputs and returned results, together with engine or workflow identifiers and timestamps. They can also update the shared account's profile, history, usage, XP, and phase. Authorized operators with backend access can access this shared storage. Persistence is asynchronous: a successful calculation is not confirmation that a record was saved.

The connector exposes no personal history, account administration, export, or deletion tool. The separate Vedic D1/D9 chart handler does not itself save a reading-history record; this limited route behavior is not a promise of end-to-end no-storage processing.

Processing services

A tool request passes from ChatGPT/OpenAI through the Cloudflare sponsored MCP Worker to the Railway-hosted Selemene API and its calculation services. Backend PostgreSQL storage and Redis/cache infrastructure can process or retain calculation information. These website pages are hosted on Vercel.

Your ChatGPT conversation and account are governed separately by the host's privacy policy and controls. Selemene cannot delete that conversation through a tool or an email request to the publisher. See OpenAI's privacy policy and the privacy information applicable to your host account.

Cloudflare processes connection information at its edge; see Cloudflare's privacy policy. Provider operational logs and backups have their own arrangements. This notice does not establish a complete provider-region inventory or a verified lifetime for every provider log and backup.

Abuse controls and logs

To enforce service limits, the Worker processes Cloudflare's trusted connecting IP and derives a salted hash. Its distributed counter store holds hashes, counters, and timestamps, with at most 512 active hash entries. Inactive entries are pruned using a two-minute inactivity period during requests and scheduled alarms. The service-wide daily aggregate resets by UTC day. Shared networks and ChatGPT gateways can share an IP; this is not personal account tracking.

The reviewed Worker does not log or persist raw IP addresses, reading inputs or results, or sponsor credentials, and its Worker observability is disabled. This does not establish a system-wide absence of logging. The backend logs request metadata such as method, path, trace and shared-account identifiers, response status, and duration.

The backend also has error monitoring configured. Its complete delivered payloads, provider retention, and backup lifetimes have not been verified. Do not assume that disabling the connector's own reading logs eliminates infrastructure processing.

Retention and data requests

There is no fixed automatic deletion period currently established for shared reading, profile, or usage records. The inspected service has no scheduled purge for those records or general customer reading-deletion endpoint. Information may therefore persist after a reading or after you remove the plugin.

For an access or deletion request, email sheshnarayan.iyer@gmail.com. Start with the action or tool used, an approximate timestamp, and a returned request or reading identifier if one is available. Send only the minimum information needed to locate the relevant record; do not send credentials, a full birth chart, or a complete private reading in your initial message.

The publisher reviews which records can be identified and what action is feasible, then responds with the scope of that review. Because this anonymous connector uses shared storage without a linked personal account, an individual request may not reliably identify every record. No automatic erasure, guaranteed completion time, or complete backup deletion is promised here. Removing the plugin is not a deletion receipt for backend records or host conversations.

Website visits and updates

These Selemene pages do not provide a birth-data submission form or run a calculation when you visit. Their assets and navigation are served as website content; the hosting provider still processes normal web requests. Following a link to another website or sending an email uses that service's own arrangements.

Updates to this notice are published on this page with a revised date. Questions about the scope or current handling of your information can be sent to sheshnarayan.iyer@gmail.com.
