Chapter one · Accurate input preparation

State your inputs. Name what is missing.

Preparation restates the reading you have asked for and the inputs it needs. If a required field is unknown — a birth time, for example — Selemene will identify it as missing rather than guess. You can supply the field, or choose a reading that does not require it.

Chapter two · Explicitly requested readings

A reading runs only when you ask.

Discussion and preparation alone do not run a calculation. Before you request an ordinary reading, the host presents the shared-storage disclosure. The free connector needs no personal Selemene login, API key, or OAuth approval; its sponsor credential stays on the server.

Chapter three · Values vs. reflection

Calculated values, then symbolic reflection.

Selemene returns calculated values, local draws, and source text with their limits. ChatGPT writes interpretation and final prose under your applicable host limits, using Aletheios and Pichet as complementary writing perspectives. This connector invokes no additional operator-funded narration model. Readings offer symbolic reflection, not professional advice or guaranteed outcomes.

Chapter four · Persistence & your agency

Understand shared storage. Choose your next step.

Free readings use a shared Selemene administrator account. Birth inputs and calculated results may be stored there; usage, profile, XP and phase may change. There is no separate private user history. Saving is asynchronous; a successful reply is not a storage receipt. Read the privacy notice before requesting a reading.
