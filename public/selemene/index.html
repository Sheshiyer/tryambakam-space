Selemene Engine · Tryambakam Noesis

A companion for symbolic reflection.

Selemene helps you discover supported engines, prepare accurate inputs, and request a reading you have explicitly asked for — with calculated values kept clearly separate from interpretation. Its ChatGPT integration is in preparation.

Scroll

Chapter one · Accurate input preparation

State your inputs. Name what is missing.

Preparation restates the reading you have asked for and the inputs it needs. If a required field is unknown — a birth time, for example — Selemene will identify it as missing rather than guess. You can supply the field, or choose a reading that does not require it.

Chapter two · Explicitly requested readings

A reading runs only when you ask.

No calculation runs on Selemene's own initiative. A reading executes only after you explicitly request it with the prepared inputs. That request is the point at which values are computed and, if the workflow does so, saved to your account.

Chapter three · Values vs. reflection

Calculated values, then symbolic reflection.

What the engine returns is shown as values. Any interpretation offered on top of those values is symbolic reflection, not instruction. Selemene does not provide medical, psychological, financial, or legal advice, and does not guarantee outcomes.

Chapter four · Persistence & your agency

Your account, your record, your next step.

Requested calculations can save the supplied inputs and returned results to your Selemene account and update profile, history, usage, XP or phase as designed by the engine invoked. Saving is asynchronous — a successful reply does not by itself guarantee the record was saved.
